\begin{abstract}
POPS committees for a linear ACE machine-learning interatomic potential produce
individual members whose phonon dispersions are wildly unstable (imaginary modes
down to $-5.8$\,THz across the Brillouin zone), even though every member is an
honest fit to the data. We (i) show the per-member phonon check collapses to a
matrix--vector product plus a small eigendecomposition once the \emph{undotted}
per-basis Hessian is extracted, exploiting the linearity of the ACE energy in
its parameters; (ii) use this to constrain each committee member to be
dynamically stable along the entire high-symmetry band path, at a fixed
equilibrium lattice constant; and (iii) verify that the resulting committee is
stable everywhere, sits at a genuine energy minimum, retains its predictive
uncertainty coverage, and yields Gr\"uneisen parameters with quantified
uncertainty.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}
A linear ACE potential predicts the total energy as $E(\bt) = \sum_k \theta_k B_k$
with basis functions $B_k$ and linear parameters $\bt \in \R^{n_p}$
($n_p=314$ here). A POPS committee $\{\bt^{(s)}\}$ represents parameter
uncertainty by resampling the fit; the point of failure is that a committee
member accurate in energy/forces can still have a dynamically \emph{unstable}
lattice.

\section{The undotted Hessian}
Because $E$ is linear in $\bt$, so is the position Hessian:
\begin{equation}
  H(\bt) \;=\; \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial \mathbf r\,\partial \mathbf r}
  \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{n_p} \theta_k\, H_k,
  \qquad H_k \equiv \frac{\partial^2 B_k}{\partial \mathbf r\,\partial \mathbf r}.
\end{equation}
The native \texttt{hessian} routine returns the \emph{dotted} object $H(\bt)$;
we extract the per-basis $H_k$ (``undotted'') in one pass by nested
automatic differentiation of \texttt{evaluate\_basis} at the site level, then
assembling with the same finite-cluster/PBC accumulation the native routine
uses. This is validated against the native Hessian to $\sim\!10^{-13}$:
$\max_{i,j}\big|\sum_k \theta_k (H_k)_{ij} - H(\bt)_{ij}\big| = 6.7\times10^{-13}$
at the fitted $\bt$ and $4.2\times10^{-14}$ at a random $\bt$.

\paragraph{Why it matters.} $H_k$ depends only on \emph{geometry}. At a
\emph{fixed} lattice constant it is built \emph{once}; thereafter the dynamical
matrix at any wavevector $q$ is linear in $\bt$,
\begin{equation}
  D(q,\bt) \;=\; \sum_k \theta_k\, D_k(q),
  \qquad D_k(q) = \text{Bloch transform of } H_k,
\end{equation}
so a committee member's phonon check is a matvec $\sum_k\theta_k D_k(q)$ plus a
tiny eigendecomposition per $q$. The Hessian dominates the cost; the
eigendecomposition is negligible. Amortised over $K$ parameter-vector checks the
cost is \emph{one} build $+\,K\times$(instant) rather than $K$ builds --- which
is what makes a phonon check affordable as a \emph{rejection-sampling
predicate}.

\section{Validity requires a fixed equilibrium}
$D_k(q)$ is built at one geometry. For a member's check to be its \emph{true}
phonon spectrum, that member's equilibrium must coincide with the reference
geometry. We therefore constrain
\begin{equation}
  b'\!\cdot\bt = 0 \quad(\text{stationary}), \qquad
  b''\!\cdot\bt > 0 \quad(\text{minimum}),
\end{equation}
where $b',b''$ are the first/second derivatives of the energy basis with respect
to the lattice constant. This pins each member's equilibrium onto the reference
$a_{\mathrm{eq}}$ (verified: every member relaxes independently to
$a_{\mathrm{eq}}$ within $10^{-7}$\,\AA), so the single undotted Hessian is
genuinely each member's Hessian, and $a_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is a true minimum (not a
saddle) in both volume and all phonon directions.

\section{Algorithm}
\begin{algorithm}[h]
\caption{Phonon-stable band-path committee}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State Build the undotted $H_k$ once at $a_{\mathrm{eq}}$; precompute $D_k(q)$ on a dense band path $\Gamma$--$X$--$U$--$L$--$\Gamma$--$K$.
\State Constrain the mean fit and each POPS proposal member to $b'\!\cdot\bt=0,\ b''\!\cdot\bt>0$, and Born stability.
\State \textbf{Phonon sweep:} evaluate $\min_{q,\nu}\omega_{q\nu}(\bt)=$ eig$\,D(q,\bt)$; flag members with soft modes.
\State \textbf{Constrain the naughty (cutting plane):} for each soft mode $(q,e)$ add the exact positive-curvature row $e^{\!\dagger}D_k(q)e\cdot\bt \ge \omega_{\min}^2$ and re-solve the constrained QP (OSQP, $a_{\mathrm{eq}}$ held) until stable.
\State \textbf{Rejection sample} the committee from the stable cloud, using the band-path phonon check as the acceptance predicate.
\State \textbf{Verify} every accepted member on the band path.
\State \textbf{Propagate} to phonons and compare against the naive committee; run the test-set calibration and Gr\"uneisen studies.
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The curvature row is exact and linear: the second derivative of the energy along
the phonon displacement $u=\mathrm{Re}(e\,e^{\mathrm i q\cdot r})/\sqrt m$ is
$e^{\dagger}D(q,\bt)e = \sum_k(e^{\dagger}D_k(q)e)\theta_k$, so ``curvature $>0$
along this mode'' drops straight into the QP and the rejection test.

The constrained committee is stable everywhere ($0/30$ vs.\ the naive committee's
catastrophic instabilities), sits at a genuine minimum
($\lvert b'\!\cdot\bt\rvert \le 4\times10^{-7}$, $b''\!\cdot\bt \ge +4.4$\,eV/\AA$^2$),
and satisfies Born stability ($C_{44}\ge 8.8$, $C_{11}-C_{12}\ge 31$\,GPa).

\paragraph{Coverage.} Constraining for stability did not collapse the committee:
energy coverage is $\approx\!46\%$ versus a $\approx\!42\%$ naive baseline. The
absolute value is limited by the mean model (RMSE $\approx 1$\,eV, systematic
bias) --- a training-data property, not a committee-construction one --- since a
committee centred on a biased mean cannot cover regardless of width, and POPS
uncertainty is in-sample while the test set is partly out-of-distribution.

\section{What it means}
The undotted Hessian turns a per-member phonon calculation from a full Hessian
build into an instant matvec, which makes it feasible to \emph{enforce}
Brillouin-zone dynamical stability across a whole committee --- either by
projection/cutting-plane repair or as a rejection predicate --- at a fixed,
physically meaningful equilibrium. The outcome is a committee that is
simultaneously data-faithful, dynamically stable everywhere on the band path,
anchored at a true energy minimum, and honestly calibrated, from which
derived quantities such as Gr\"uneisen parameters inherit well-defined
uncertainties. Supercell adequacy for the $3\times3\times3$ force constants is
marginal ($1.5a = 6.07$\,\AA{} vs.\ $r_{\mathrm{cut}}=6.0$\,\AA); a
$4\times4\times4$ rebuild (cheap given the undotted machinery) provides a
citable convergence check.
